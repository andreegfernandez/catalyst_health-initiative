03 / Our story

Built for work
that matters.

Catalyst was created to bring rigorous analytical skills into contact with real healthcare questions.

Founded by Agatha Tyas, Odyssefs Botsis and Andree Fernandez while studying Health Economics, Policy and Management at Karolinska Institutet, Catalyst grew from a shared belief that professional development is strongest when the work carries real responsibility.

Academic cases build knowledge. Real healthcare questions also demand judgement, accountability and clear communication. We therefore approach selected projects as professional engagements: defining the question with our partners, applying appropriate methods, critically reviewing the evidence and communicating what the results mean — and what they do not.

Our aim is twofold: create useful work for organisations tackling meaningful healthcare challenges, and build an environment where emerging professionals can apply their expertise with the standards expected beyond the classroom.
