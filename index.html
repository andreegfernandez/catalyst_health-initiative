03 / Our story

We created
the opportunity
we were looking for.

Catalyst began with a shared ambition: to make meaningful work more accessible to students.

Agatha Tyas, Odyssefs Botsis and Andree Fernandez founded Catalyst while studying Health Economics, Policy and Management at Karolinska Institutet. They wanted opportunities to apply their knowledge to real data, real organisations and real healthcare decisions.

Selected partners receive research and practical advice pro bono. Students gain responsibility, experience and connections through projects with the potential to create value for healthcare and patients.
